<div class="research-profile-intro">
  <p>My current work studies memory for AI agents, efficient and reliable multi-agent systems, and reasoning. At the <a href="https://declare-lab.github.io/">DeCLaRe Lab</a>, we work on safety, trustworthiness, multimodality, AI for science, efficiency, and embodied AI.</p>
  <p>My earlier research focused on multimodal representation learning, affective computing, and sentiment analysis.</p>
